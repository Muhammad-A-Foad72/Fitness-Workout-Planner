Bent-Over Row

  • Muscle group: Back
  • Equipment: Barbell
  • Difficulty: Intermediate

How to do it

  1. 1Hold the bar with an overhand grip, hands slightly wider than your shoulders.
  2. 2Hinge at your hips until your back is at about 45 degrees, knees slightly bent.
  3. 3Let the bar hang under your shoulders with your arms straight and your back flat.
  4. 4Pull the bar to your lower ribs, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  5. 5Lower it with control until your arms are straight again.

Safety tips

  • Keep your back flat, and end the set if it starts to round.
  • Do not jerk the weight up with your hips.
  • Start light, and stop if you feel sharp pain in your lower back.
