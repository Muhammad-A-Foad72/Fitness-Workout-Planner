Push-Up

  • Muscle group: Chest
  • Equipment: Bodyweight
  • Difficulty: Beginner

How to do it

  1. 1Start in a high plank with your hands under your shoulders, a little wider than shoulder width.
  2. 2Make a straight line from head to heels by squeezing your glutes and bracing your core.
  3. 3Bend your elbows to lower your chest, keeping them at about 45 degrees from your body.
  4. 4Stop when your chest is about a fist's height above the floor.
  5. 5Press the floor away to straighten your arms, breathing out as you rise.

Safety tips

  • Keep your hips level, not sagging or raised.
  • Put your hands on a bench or a wall if full push-ups are too hard.
  • Stop if you feel pain in your wrists or shoulders.
